Privacy Policy

eBay AI Commerce

Effective Date: November 10, 2026

1. Introduction

eBay AI Commerce is a private seller automation tool designed to help its operator manage an authorized eBay seller account.

We respect user privacy and aim to protect personal information processed through the tool.

2. Information We Process

When connected to eBay with the account owner's permission, the application may process:

  • eBay account authorization information
  • Product listings and inventory information
  • Order information when order features are enabled
  • Shipping and fulfillment information when required

3. How Information Is Used

Information is used to support the authorized seller's business operations, including inventory monitoring, order management, and fulfillment when these features are enabled.

Information is not used for unrelated advertising or sold to data brokers.

4. eBay Authorization

The application uses eBay's official OAuth authorization system.

The seller grants access through eBay and can revoke application access through their eBay account settings.

The application does not require the seller to provide their eBay password to the operator.

5. Data Security

The application is designed to encrypt stored OAuth refresh tokens and restrict access to authorized application services.

Cloudflare infrastructure is used for application processing and protected data storage.

6. Information Sharing

Information may be shared with eBay or necessary service providers to deliver authorized features.

When fulfillment services are enabled, necessary delivery details may be provided to contracted fulfillment partners to complete orders.

Personal information is not sold.

7. Data Retention

Information is retained only as needed for authorized operations, security, or applicable legal requirements.

Verified privacy and deletion requests will be reviewed and addressed in accordance with applicable obligations.

8. Your Privacy Choices

The account owner may revoke eBay authorization through eBay's account settings.

Individuals may request access, correction, or deletion of applicable personal information, subject to legal requirements.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or requests relating to an eBay transaction, contact the seller through eBay Messages associated with that transaction.

10. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the application's capabilities or data handling practices change.
